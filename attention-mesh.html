HAProxy terminates TLS using the CA certificate you installed at enrollment. Inspects the stream, identifies ad requests by domain and payload pattern, and hands them to the swap engine. WHY CONSENT MAKES THIS LEGAL AND VALUABLE

Without your consent, a transparent TLS-intercepting proxy is a malicious MITM attack. With your explicit, informed consent — on your own devices, your own network — it's a network appliance you own. The law has been clear on this since ECPA. The ad premium for verified, consented impressions exists for the same reason: accountability. An anonymous impression could be a bot. A consented one is you. That distinction is worth $2.50 per thousand more, minimum.

You are about to install a certificate authority on this device. This certificate allows the AttentionMesh proxy to inspect encrypted HTTPS traffic on this network. This is how we replace surveillance ads with clean, consented ones that pay you.

What this means: The proxy can read the content of HTTPS requests on this network, including page content. Banking, healthcare, and financial apps that use certificate pinning will bypass the proxy automatically and are not affected. Your passwords and messages in those apps are not visible to the proxy.

What we do with it: Identify and replace ad slots. We do not log, store, or transmit your browsing content. No data leaves this network node without your explicit authorization.
